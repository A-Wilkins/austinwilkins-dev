01 About

I like problems where the details actually matter.

Most of my work sits where a system meets the real world: a platform that has to state facts about real property, a site that has to load fast on a phone in a parking lot, a pipeline that has to be right about which job is worth applying to. That constraint is the interesting part, and it is what I optimize for.

I work across the stack — Python for services, automation, and data work, .NET and ASP.NET Core on enterprise identity platforms, JavaScript at the edge on Cloudflare Workers, and enough front-end craft that the things I ship look finished rather than functional. I care about correctness, about not inventing data, and about leaving a codebase someone else can pick up.
